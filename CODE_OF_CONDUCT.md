# Code of conduct

We want HiveLab 合屋 to be a friendly place to work on. Everyone taking part in this project's issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good faith, whatever someone's background or experience;
- give and take feedback on the work, not the person;
- not harass, insult, discriminate against or threaten anyone, and not publish other people's private information;
- keep discussions on topic and follow the maintainers' reasonable requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub.

本项目欢迎所有人参与。请互相尊重，对事不对人；不得骚扰、歧视、威胁他人，或公开他人的隐私信息。维护者可以删除不当内容、关闭相关讨论或限制违规者参与。

This text follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
